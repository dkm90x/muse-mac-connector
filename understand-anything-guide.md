# HOW TO UNDERSTAND ANYTHING
### A five-move reference for breaking down any topic

---

## MOVE 1 — PURPOSE
**Ask: what job does this thing do in the world?**
- Not "what is it" — "what does it do"
- Music moves emotion through organized sound
- Law resolves conflict through agreed rules
- Every topic has ONE core job. Find it first.

> If you can't state the purpose in one sentence, you don't understand it yet.

---

## MOVE 2 — STRUCTURE
**Break it into the fewest parts that still work.**
- If removing a part kills the thing → it's essential
- If removing it just makes it weaker → it's supporting
- If removing it changes nothing → it's decoration
- Most topics have **3 to 7** essential parts. If you have 20, you're listing features, not structure.

---

## MOVE 3 — DYNAMICS
**How do the parts move and interact?**
- What flows between them? (information, energy, money, attention)
- What causes what? Map the feedback loops.
- Where does it start, where does it end, what cycles?

> Structure is the skeleton. Dynamics is the blood.

---

## MOVE 4 — FORMS
**What are the different ways the same function shows up?**
- Separate the *invariant* (the job, always the same) from the *variant* (the packaging, always different)
- A restaurant and a food truck both serve food. Different forms, same function.
- Edge cases are just unusual forms — not exceptions to the rule.

---

## MOVE 5 — TRACE ONE THREAD
**Pick one essential part and follow it end to end.**
- Start at the input. Follow every transformation. End at the output.
- This is where abstract understanding becomes real understanding.
- One thread traced deeply beats five threads skimmed.

---

## THE META-RULE
**Purpose → Structure → Dynamics → Forms → One Thread**

Repeat at every level. A part of the topic has its own purpose, structure, dynamics. Zoom in, run the five moves again. Zoom out, same thing.

You're not memorizing a topic. You're learning to see the shape of any topic.

---
*Save this. Use it when something feels too big to learn.*
